Your remote

The bridge copies your hand remote, so the blinds accept it like a second remote. Nothing on the motors changes.

  1. 1
    Press Start learning
  2. 2
    Hold the remote near the bridge and press Up
  3. 3
    Press Down
  4. 4
    Press Stop (the middle button)

Other remotes (a second hand remote or a wall switch): the bridge follows their presses too, so positions stay right.

Channels and blinds

Pair motors to the bridge Experimental

Gives the bridge its own remote ID for a channel, so that motor keeps working from Home Assistant even if the hand remote is lost or replaced. The hand remote keeps working too. Not yet tested on every motor: if the motor doesn't jog at the end, nothing has changed.

Range

Frequency scan: hold any button on the hand remote near the bridge, then press Scan (about 4 seconds). The bridge tunes to where the remote is strongest.

Test a channel

Send a command on a channel to see which blind moves.

A blind on more than one channel is a group: one command moves them all.

Scenes

A scene moves several blinds to set positions in one go, for example Movie or Morning. Each scene also shows up in Home Assistant.

Location

Needed for schedules at sunrise or sunset. Only the bridge uses it.

Schedules

Schedules run on the bridge itself, so they still work when Home Assistant or the internet is down. Locked blinds are skipped.

Locks, windows and wind

Lock a blind from its ⋯ menu or its Lock switch in Home Assistant. Home Assistant, scenes and schedules then leave it alone. The hand remote and Stop still work.

Window open: publish ON from a door or window sensor automation and that blind won't close (it stops if it's closing). OFF when it's shut.

Wind: publish ON from a wind sensor and every blind set to Awning retracts and stays put until OFF.
